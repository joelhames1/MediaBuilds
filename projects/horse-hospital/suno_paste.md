# There's a Horse Loose in a Hospital

## Style
```
Broadway rock-opera showstopper, 124 BPM, 4/4, major key, key change up a whole step for the final chorus.
Male lead tenor, bright and wry with crisp musical-theater diction: tender and half-spoken over solo piano in the intro, fast conversational patter in the verses, full belt in the choruses. Ensemble gang vocals shout the call-and-response lines.
Grand piano leads the intro and bridge. Choruses: driving rock drums, electric guitar, brass stabs, big strings, choir.
Hard texture changes between sections: the band cuts to dead silence for the whispered break; stomping drums and a distorted guitar riff in the instrumental break.
Ends on solo piano and a soft held vocal note.
```

## Style (terse fallback)
```
broadway rock opera, theatrical pop rock, musical theater male tenor, fast patter verses, gang vocals, piano, brass, strings, choir, key change, comedic
```

## Exclude Styles

## Settings

## Notes
Concept: a Broadway rock-opera showstopper sung with total sincerity about a horse loose in a hospital. The joke is the commitment. The singer is the narrator; the Horse, the Hippo, the news anchor and the bird-in-the-airport expert are voiced separately (ElevenLabs) and spliced into the gaps in post, so Suno only has to nail one voice.

What matters in a take, in order: (1) every word of the patter verses is intelligible, because comedy dies in mush; (2) the chorus hook "There's a horse loose in a hospital" is big and singable; (3) the "I'M A HORSE!" gang shout; (4) the dry stop-time "you dumb fucking horse"; (5) a real silence after "Gary Busey..." and a lift into the last chorus; (6) a clean solo-piano ending. Clarity beats vocal beauty. Don't chase the lounge-piano brunch switch or the stomping instrumental texture if the rest is right; I can fix texture in post with stems.

Expect about 3:30. Explore with Max Mode off, then turn it on for the keeper (long song, v6 quality dips after ~2 min).

If it misses:
- Patter turns to mush: drop to 116 BPM in the Style field, nothing else.
- Suno refuses or censors a line: change "fucking" to "freaking" (it gets bleeped in the video anyway) and "Gary Busey" to "a stranger in a van".
- Vocals land on the Instrumental Break: fine, I'll strip them with stem separation.
- Runs past 3:50: cut Verse 5 (brunch) first, then the Outro.
- Long style prompt comes back mushy: try the terse fallback once.

Handoff: download only the keeper (WAV if your plan offers it, MP3 is fine), and tell me if you edited any lyrics in Suno.
